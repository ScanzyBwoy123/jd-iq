# JD IQ
## Autonomous Scientific Intelligence and Discovery System

JD IQ is a long-term scientific intelligence and discovery project designed to research, investigate, model, experiment, learn and improve its scientific capabilities through controlled and evidence-based processes.

## Mission

Develop an extensible scientific intelligence system that can:

- Conduct multidisciplinary scientific research.
- Discover and investigate scientific knowledge gaps.
- Formulate and evaluate hypotheses.
- Design computational experiments and simulations.
- Develop and evaluate scientific models.
- Verify findings using reproducible evidence.
- Improve its learning and research capabilities through controlled evaluation.
- Maintain a traceable history of research, experiments and discoveries.

## Governance

JD IQ follows these principles:

1. Research before implementation.
2. Evidence before conclusions.
3. Reproducibility and independent verification.
4. Clear separation of facts, hypotheses, predictions and simulations.
5. Documented failures and limitations.
6. Controlled and reversible self-improvement.
7. Scientific integrity, safety and accountability.
8. No unsupported claims of scientific breakthroughs.

## Investigation Programme

The approved programme contains 200 numbered scientific investigations.

Investigation expansion is closed at #200 unless explicitly reopened.

Historical records with missing descriptions must be recovered from their original sources. Missing information must never be fabricated.

## Core Architecture

JD IQ is designed around eight primary subsystems:

1. Intelligence Core
2. Research Intelligence
3. Knowledge System
4. Discovery Intelligence
5. Experimental Laboratory
6. Verification Intelligence
7. Learning and Evolution
8. Autonomous Operations
